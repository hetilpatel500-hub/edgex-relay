Missed-Call Text-Back Setup Guide

A step-by-step written guide to building your own missed-call text-back with Twilio and Make.com. When you can't answer, the caller gets a text from your business within about a minute. You keep your existing phone number.

What it is, honestly

  • A written guide (PDF, 14 pages, plus a Markdown copy). You create the accounts and click through the settings yourself.
  • Not a one-click import, not a hosted service, and not software we run for you.
  • Built for US and Canadian numbers, with notes for the UK and EU.
  • Not legal advice. It explains the texting rules in plain language, but you're responsible for how you use texting.

How the finished setup works

  1. A call is missed

    Your phone forwards unanswered or busy calls to a Twilio number you own.

  2. A short greeting

    The caller hears that you're texting them now. Optional voicemail.

  3. The text goes out

    A Make.com scenario checks the number, skips duplicates, and sends your message.

  4. Replies reach you

    If they text back, the reply is forwarded to your mobile.

What's inside

  1. Setting up Twilio and choosing a number
  2. Registering for business texting (US A2P 10DLC), which you should start first
  3. The call greeting, with an optional voicemail version
  4. Building the Make.com scenario module by module, including duplicate protection, a "don't text again within 12 hours" rule, and automatic retries
  5. Connecting your Twilio number to the scenario
  6. Forwarding missed calls from your business line (VoIP, mobile and landline)
  7. Getting customer replies on your own phone
  8. An eight-point test checklist
  9. A troubleshooting table
  10. Texting rules in plain language

Plus ready-to-edit message templates, a quick-reference card, and an alternative way to send the text if the Twilio module in Make changes.

What you'll need

  • A business phone line that can forward calls when you don't answer or are busy (most mobile carriers and business phone systems can)
  • A Twilio account and a Make.com account (both have free starting options)
  • About 60 to 90 minutes, plus waiting time for US texting registration, which can take days to weeks
  • A second phone for testing
