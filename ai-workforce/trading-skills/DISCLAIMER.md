- **History is not a forecast.** Backtests, simulated paths and "odds" describe how prices
  behaved in the past on the data you supplied. Markets change. Simulated results have
  limits: they assume fills that real markets may not give, and they can't account for
  every cost, liquidity condition or event.

  afford to lose, and understand your broker's rules, including margin and
