## Read first (5 rules that protect the account)

1. **The account is yours, in your real name.** Fiverr checks your
   government ID. The name, address and tax info you enter must match it
   exactly. Buyers never see your legal name; they see the display name
   "Edgex". One person, one account: never open a second one.
2. **Your username is permanent.** It becomes fiverr.com/username. Pick
   carefully (options in Step 2). Don't use your real name in it.
3. **AI use is disclosed in every gig.** Fiverr allows AI but says it
   must support real skill, work must be customized, and hiding how work
   was made can get the account suspended. So every gig says it plainly,
   and every order asks the buyer to confirm AI-assisted work is OK.
4. **You read every delivery before you click Deliver.** The gigs
   promise "reviewed by a person before delivery." That person is you.
   It takes 5-10 minutes per order with the checklist in FULFILLMENT.md.
   Don't skip it: it's what keeps us inside Fiverr's AI rule.
5. **All talk stays on Fiverr.** No emails, phone numbers or outside
   links in messages. Agents draft replies; you paste them.

Money facts: Fiverr keeps 20% of each order. Your share clears 14 days
after the order completes, then you can withdraw.

Gig limit: sources disagree. Most 2026 guides say new sellers get 7
active gigs; one Fiverr Help Center snippet said 4. Starting with 4 fits
under either number and keeps the workload manageable. Check your own
limit in Fiverr after signup (Gigs page) before adding more.
