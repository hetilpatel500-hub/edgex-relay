## 1. Fernwick Linen Table Runner, Oat

**SEO title (57 chars):** Linen Table Runner in Oat, Washed & Soft | Fernwick Goods

**Target keyword:** linen table runner

**Description**

A plain table can feel unfinished. This runner fixes that in one move.

Our Oat runner is stone-washed linen, so it arrives soft and relaxed, not
stiff. The warm, neutral tone sits well with wood, white, or black tables,
and it gets softer every time you wash it.

- **Size:** 14 x 72 in, fits most 4 to 6 seat tables
- **Material:** 100% linen, stone-washed
- **Care:** machine wash cold, tumble dry low
- **Finish:** hemmed edges, no fringe to snag

Lay it straight down the middle for everyday meals, or fold it in thirds
across a side table. Either way, it's the easiest upgrade in the room.
