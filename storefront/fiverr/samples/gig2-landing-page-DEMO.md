## The problem

You started freelancing to do the work you're good at. Not to chase
receipts at midnight.

- Transactions pile up across two bank accounts and a card.
- You're never sure what you can spend, or what to set aside for tax.
- Every April starts with a shoebox and a sinking feeling.

## How it works

1. **Connect.** Give read-only access to your bank and card accounts.
2. **We sort.** Every transaction is categorized each month.
3. **You see it.** A one-page summary lands in your inbox by the 10th.

## What you get each month

- Categorized transactions, reconciled to your statements
- Profit and loss summary in plain English
- A "set aside for tax" estimate to discuss with your accountant
- Year-end file ready for your tax preparer

## Who it's for

Freelancers, consultants and creatives with one business and no
bookkeeper. If you have payroll or inventory, we'll tell you on the call
if we're not the right fit.

## Pricing

**One flat monthly price, [price set by client].** No hourly bills.

## FAQ

**Do you file my taxes?** No. We keep the books; your tax preparer
files. We hand them a clean year-end file.

**Is my data safe?** We use read-only access. We can't move money.

**Can I cancel?** Yes, any month, with no fee.

## Final call to action

### Spend April on anything but receipts.

**[Book a free 20-minute call]**
